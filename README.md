### 📊 Project Overview
This project analyzes EV charging behavior, revenue generation, station usage and energy consumption using Microsoft Excel. The analysis was performed on **6,500 EV charging sessions**, generating over **2.26M in total revenue** and providing insights into customer behavior, charging trends and operational performance.
The dashboard helps stakeholders identify high-performing cities and vehicle categories, optimize charging infrastructure and support data-driven business decisions.

---

### 🎯 Objectives
* Analyze EV charging data to identify revenue and usage trends.
* Compare charging performance across vehicle categories and cities.
* Evaluate energy consumption behavior over time.
* Assess customer satisfaction through ratings analysis.
* Generate actionable recommendations for business growth.
* Present insights through an interactive Excel dashboard.

---

### 💡 Key Insights
* Generated over **2.26M+ revenue** across **6,500 charging sessions** with an average energy consumption of **31 kWh** per session.
* **3W EVs** generated the highest revenue at approximately **583K+**, outperforming 2W EVs, 4W EVs and Electric Buses.
* **Kolkata** generated the highest charging revenue at approximately **308K+**, followed by Hyderabad (**295K+**) and Pune (**279K+**).
* Charging activity was distributed across Fast, Standard and Super Fast charging stations, indicating demand for multiple charging options.
* Energy consumption fluctuated throughout the year, reflecting changing charging demand and customer charging behavior.
* The average customer rating was **3/5**, highlighting opportunities to improve customer experience and service quality.

---

### 📌 Recommendations
* Expand charging infrastructure in high-revenue cities such as Kolkata, Hyderabad and Pune.
* Increase investment in Fast and Super Fast charging stations to meet growing customer demand.
* Introduce customer loyalty programs and promotional campaigns to improve customer retention.
* Monitor charging demand patterns to optimize energy distribution and station availability.
* Improve customer experience through better maintenance, reduced waiting times and enhanced customer support.
* Focus on 3W EV charging solutions as they represent the highest revenue-generating vehicle segment.
